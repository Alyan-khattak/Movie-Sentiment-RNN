This film is an absolute masterpiece. The performances are breathtaking, the cinematography is stunning, and the story moved me deeply. One of the best films I have seen in years. A bold and visually stunning achievement that pushes boundaries in ways few directors dare. I was completely captivated from the first scene to the last.

A terrible waste of two hours. The plot made absolutely no sense and the acting was wooden throughout. The dialogue was cringe-worthy and the pacing was painfully slow. I cannot believe this got made. The director had no idea what he was doing and it showed in every single scene. Avoid at all costs.
